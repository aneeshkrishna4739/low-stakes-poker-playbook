Low-Stakes Playbook

Built to exploit a loose, passive player pool: value-heavy raises, bigger value bets, fewer bluffs, and fast folds to big raises.

Players at the table

Preflop

Opening, 3-bet, 4-bet and 5-bet ranges for every seat. They change with the number of players you set above. If you have your own opening chart, use it instead of the Open view. Sizes assume a 2.5bb open (3bb from the SB).

Postflop tree

Pick the street, your role, your position and your hand bucket. Each bucket has a bet-type name (value bet, protection bet, semi-bluff…) that means the same thing. Sizes are % of the current pot, heads-up in a single-raised pot.

Practice

Random hands at the table size you set above, from any seat. Make every decision yourself. The judge reads your hand the way this guide does, finds your bucket, and checks your choice against the charts. Bet sizes are approximate. Every hand is saved: History lists them, and Retrospect analyzes your strengths, weaknesses and leaks.

Hand buckets

Pick the vulnerability by counting the next cards that could beat you or kill your action: 0–4 is non-vulnerable (a gutshot at most), 5–9 slightly (one real draw), 10 or more very. Re-bucket on every street. A set of 7s on Q♠9♠7♦ is Nutted · very vulnerable on the flop and drops to Good · very vulnerable when the 8♠ hits.
